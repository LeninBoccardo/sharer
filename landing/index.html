Sharer GitHub

Move files. Not metadata.

Peer-to-peer file sharing on your local network. End-to-end encrypted. No accounts, no cloud, no upload limits — just your devices, talking directly.

iOS support is on the roadmap (v2). Android requires sideloading for now — not on the Play Store yet.

⚡

Direct, not detoured

Files travel device-to-device over Wi-Fi. There's no server in the middle and no upload step — what your sender's network can do, you get.

🔒

Encrypted end-to-end

Every chunk is sealed with AES-256-GCM. Devices pair once via QR code and pin each other's TLS certificate — only paired peers can complete a transfer.

🚫

No accounts. Ever.

No sign-up, no email, no recovery flow. Your phone and your laptop know each other; that's the whole authentication model.

📦

Any file, any size

Stream-encrypted in 64 KB chunks — never loaded into memory. A 4 GB video transfers like a 4 KB document, just slower.

How it works

  1. Pair once. Open both devices, scan a QR code, confirm a 6-digit fingerprint. The two devices remember each other forever.
  2. Share from anywhere. The system share menu sends files into Sharer; pick a peer, transfer starts.
  3. Stay quiet on hostile Wi-Fi. On a network you haven't trusted, Sharer goes silent — paired peers can still reach you, but nobody else can see you exist.
